# Oro Adventure Tours — Brand Guide

## Company Overview
**Oro Adventure Tours** is an adventure tourism company based in **Oroquieta City, Misamis Occidental, Mindanao, Philippines**. The company is a local pioneer in outdoor adventure experiences, offering kayaking, mangrove tours, and coastal activities in one of Mindanao's most scenic — and underrated — destinations.

- **Facebook:** facebook.com/oroadventures
- **Location:** Oroquieta City (The City of Good Life)
- **DOT Recognition:** Promoted by the Department of Tourism — Northern Mindanao

---

## Brand Identity

### Brand Name
**Oro Adventure Tours**
> "Oro" = Gold (Spanish) — evoking the golden coastlines, golden sunsets, and the golden experience of Oroquieta.

### Brand Personality
- Bold but approachable
- Nature-loving, eco-conscious
- Proudly local and Filipino
- Affordable and accessible

### Tagline Options
- *"Your Golden Adventure Awaits"*
- *"Explore More. Spend Less."*
- *"Oroquieta's Hidden Gold"*

## Visual Style

- **Photography tone:** Vibrant, saturated tropical colors — turquoise water, lush green mangroves, golden hour light
- **Overlays:** Semi-transparent dark navy or gold overlays on photos for legible text
- **Shapes:** Organic/rounded shapes, wave motifs, leaf silhouettes
- **Logo placement:** Bottom-right corner, white version on dark backgrounds
- **Mood:** Adventurous, refreshing, local pride

---

## Core Activities to Feature
1. Kayaking at Punta Blanca
2. Mangrove exploration (bamboo bridges + paddling)
3. Island coastal tours
4. Sunset kayaking experiences
5. Group / team-building packages
6. Family-friendly tours
